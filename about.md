## What is Compound Life OS?

A personal operating system, not a blog. It has six modes:

1. **Life Systems** — decision making, project management, goal tracking
2. **Experience Discovery** — curating what to read, watch, learn
3. **Deep Research** — 60-minute reconnaissance to full deep dives
4. **Deliberation** — structured decision frameworks, multi-perspective analysis
5. **Content Compounding** — turning insights into published work
6. **Monetization** — building products and revenue from knowledge
